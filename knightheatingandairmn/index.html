Frequently Asked Questions

⚔️ 🛡️ ⚔️

Have questions regarding your HVAC equipment, repair costs, or maintenance? We're here with straight, honorable answers.

How quickly can Knight Heating respond to an emergency heating or AC failure?

We provide 24-hour emergency service across Otsego, Elk River, Rogers, and neighboring communities. In freezing winter weather or excessive summer heat, emergency calls are dispatched immediately. Call us at (763) 274-9945 anytime.

Should I repair or replace my aging furnace or AC unit?

As a general rule: if your system is over 15 years old and repair costs exceed 50% of the cost of a new system, replacement is generally the smarter financial decision. Modern Trane systems use significantly less energy and include 10-year warranties. Our technicians will inspect your unit and give you an honest recommendation without high-pressure sales.

Why choose a Premier Trane Dealer?

Trane has been named America's Most Trusted HVAC Brand for 10 consecutive years. As a Premier Trane Dealer, Knight Heating meets stringent standards in factory training, customer satisfaction ratings, and installation excellence, giving you access to top-tier equipment warranties.

How do Minnesota utility rebates work?

Major local energy utilities (CenterPoint Energy, Xcel Energy, Wright-Hennepin Electric) offer direct rebates when you install qualifying high-efficiency furnaces, heat pumps, or smart controls. Knight Heating guides you through equipment qualification and provides the documentation needed for fast rebate payout.

How often should my furnace and AC be tuned up?

We recommend tuning up your furnace in early autumn before the cold sets in, and servicing your AC in spring. Regular maintenance keeps safety sensors calibrated, detects hazardous carbon monoxide cracks, preserves manufacturer warranties, and prevents up to 90% of unexpected breakdowns.
