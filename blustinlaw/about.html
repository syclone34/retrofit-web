A Legacy of Success

Blustin & Associates has more than 35 years of experience providing transaction, corporate planning, and litigation-related services to individual and business clients. We combine the deep, battle-tested knowledge of large metropolitan law firms with the agile, personal focus of an independent legal counselor.

Every client works directly with founding partner Sholly A. Blustin. Whether navigating complex franchise negotiations with automotive manufacturers, executing buy-sell agreements for closely held businesses, securing intellectual property rights, or structuring comprehensive estate and succession plans, clients receive the direct benefit of seasoned analysis and decisive execution.
