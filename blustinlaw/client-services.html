Selling Alcohol to People Under 21 — Minnesota Statutory Framework

Entering Licensed Premises

It is unlawful for a person under 21 to enter an establishment licensed to sell alcohol for the purpose of purchasing or being served alcoholic beverages. However, no city ordinance may prohibit a person 18–20 years old from entering an establishment to:

  • Perform work for the establishment, including the lawful serving of alcoholic beverages.
  • Eat food on the licensed dining premises.
  • Attend social functions in areas of the establishment where alcohol is not sold.

Unlawful Serving & Underage Violations

  • Retail Licensee Duty: Unlawful for a retail licensee to permit anyone under 21 to consume alcohol on the premises.
  • Furnishing Prohibition: Unlawful for anyone to sell, furnish, or give alcohol to a person under 21.
  • False Identification: Unlawful to lend, borrow, or knowingly permit the use of a driver's license or ID card to purchase alcohol.
  • Misrepresentation: Unlawful for a person under 21 to claim to be 21 for the purpose of obtaining alcoholic beverages.
Statutory Proof of Age (Minnesota Law Strictly Requires One of the Following):
Valid Driver's License
Valid State ID (U.S./Canada, Photo & DOB)
Valid Military I.D. (U.S. DoD)
Valid U.S. or Foreign Passport
Valid Tribal Identification

Entrepreneurial Guidance

Start-Up Business Legal Checklist & Growth Resources

In the modern business environment, having a structured legal roadmap protects early capitalization, shields personal founders from liability, and creates the foundation for sustainable venture growth.

Minnesota Start-Up Legal Checklist

1 Entity Formation & State Filings
Select between LLC, S-Corporation, C-Corporation, or Professional Firm (PLLC) based on tax pass-through efficiency and investor requirements. File Articles of Organization with the Minnesota Secretary of State.
2 Custom Operating & Shareholder Agreements
Draft governance rules including voting thresholds, capital contribution requirements, founder equity vesting schedules, and dead-lock buy-sell provisions to prevent future owner disputes.
3 Tax Registrations & Federal EIN
Secure Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS, register for Minnesota State Tax IDs (sales, withholding) with the MN Department of Revenue, and register with DEED for unemployment insurance.
4 Intellectual Property & Brand Protection
Conduct USPTO trademark clearance searches for business names and logos, register state and federal trademarks, and ensure all proprietary code, formulas, and trade secrets are legally assigned to the corporate entity.
5 Commercial Leases & Municipal Permitting
Review retail, industrial, or office leases before signing to negotiate personal guarantees, CAM adjustments, and build-out terms. Verify local municipal zoning and obtain required city/county operational permits.
6 Employment & Independent Contractor Agreements
Establish compliant employee handbooks, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), invention assignment agreements, and enforce proper worker classifications under Minnesota wage-and-hour statutes.
