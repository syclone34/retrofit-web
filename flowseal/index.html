Engineering Excellence

Why Minnesota Chooses FlowSeal

Minnesota’s extreme temperature swings cause unprotected asphalt to crack and heave within just a few seasons. We blend specialized polymers and silica sands to create an impenetrable, flexible barrier.

0% Toxic Coal Tar Emulsion

We exclusively apply custom rubberized asphalt emulsion that is non-toxic, eco-friendly, and odorless. Safe for children, pets, runoff streams, and neighborhood air quality.

Silica Sand Anti-Slip Body

Our sealcoat is blended with fine silica sand particles that provide superior wet tire grip, firm walking traction, and eliminate the slick sheen of cheap water-diluted sealants.

Hot-Pour Vulcanized Rubber

Cracks are heated and compressed with commercial hot-pour rubber sealant at 375°F. It expands and contracts with sub-zero freezes, stopping water from entering the gravel base.

Family Owned & Operated

You deal directly with skilled owners and experienced crew leaders who take personal pride in every driveway and lot. No transient crews or fly-by-night subcontractors.

The 2-Year Maintenance Rule

Sealcoating every 2 years forms an active UV barrier that stops oil stains, prevents oxidation, and keeps asphalt flexible so it doesn’t become brittle and break down.

Clean Edging & Barricades

We power-blow all debris, trim overgrowing grass edges with steel blades, hand-brush perimeter edges along concrete, and professionally barricade your driveway upon completion.

Pavement Care Timeline

Post-Application Curing Milestones

To ensure your investment cures to maximum hardness and resists tire scuffing, please follow our certified Minnesota curing schedule.

2–4 Hrs
Surface Flash-Dry

Moisture evaporates, creating a dry-to-touch surface barrier. Keep children, pets, and sprinklers away.

24 Hrs
Safe for Foot Traffic

Pedestrians, trash cans, and bicycles can cross the driveway without marking the surface.

48 Hrs
Vehicle Access Ready

Remove barricades and drive personal vehicles onto driveway. Avoid hard dry-steering when vehicle is stationary.

7–12 Days
New Paving Full Cure

For newly paved asphalt, wait 7 days to drive and 12 days to park heavy trucks, trailers, or RVs.
