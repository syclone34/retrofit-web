Player Development

PGA Master Professional Instruction

Elevate your golf game under the guidance of one of Minnesota's most decorated and respected teaching professionals.

Greg Schulze

PGA Master Professional • Director of Instruction

Greg Schulze has over 38 years and over 30,000 lessons of player development experience. In 1999, he earned the PGA of America's highest classification — PGA Master Professional, currently one of only 10 in Minnesota.

He has been named a “Top 50 Instructor in America” by Golf Range Magazine and featured among “Best Teachers in your State” by Golf Digest for 14 consecutive years. Greg is the only professional in Minnesota golf history to receive all three prestigious awards: Teacher of the Year, Youth Development Leader, and Player Development Award.
