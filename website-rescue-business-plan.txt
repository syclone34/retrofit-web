SECTION 1: BUSINESS POSITIONING
================================================================================

RetroFit Web Design specializes in fast, affordable, high-impact website modernization for small service businesses. The core value proposition is simple:

"Your website doesn’t need a rebuild — it needs a rescue."

Instead of selling full custom redesigns that cost thousands and take weeks, the business focuses on fixing outdated, broken, slow, or poorly designed websites and turning them into clean, modern, high-converting sites within 48–72 hours.

This service fills a gap between DIY website builders and expensive marketing agencies. It is ideal for small business owners who need immediate improvements without the hassle or high cost of starting over from scratch.

SECTION 5: WORKFLOW AND OPERATIONS
================================================================================

Step 1: Rapid Site Audit
- Run prospective site through RetroFit Analyzer tool.
- Identify 5–10 high-impact visual, speed, and mobile flaws.

Step 2: Simple Pitch & Proposal
- Send personalized before/after screenshot teaser or short audit clip.
- Present the $299 Website Rescue flat-rate proposal with 48–72h guarantee.

Step 3: Rescue Sprint Execution
- Backup existing website files and content.
- Migrate text, logos, and images to modular modernization template.
- Implement responsive layout, clean fonts, click-to-call buttons, and clean CSS.
- Compress all media assets for lightning-fast mobile loading.

Step 4: Client Delivery & Approval
- Present live preview link to client for feedback.
- Make final minor polish edits within 24 hours.

Step 5: Onboarding to Monthly Care Plan
- Transfer hosting/DNS settings to secure Cloudflare/hosting pipeline.
- Enroll client into the $49/mo Maintenance & Hosting recurring billing plan.
