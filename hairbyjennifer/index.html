Visiting the Studio

What to Expect at Jennifer's Home Studio

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit to an intimate, private home hair suite.

Where is the studio located, and when do I get the address?

+

The studio is operated from Jennifer's private residential home in a peaceful, welcoming neighborhood. To protect client comfort and home privacy, the full street address and simple arrival directions are texted directly to your phone upon appointment confirmation.

Where should I park when I arrive?

+

Convenient, free parking is available right in the private driveway or directly along the quiet residential street curb. No parking meters, crowded commercial ramps, or long walks.

What is the environment like inside?

+

It is a dedicated, fully equipped professional salon suite within the home—featuring a professional wash basin, hydraulic styling chair, sanitized tools, flattering lighting, and premium products. You get all the amenities of a high-end salon without the loud dryers, overlapping clients, or salon rush.

Can I bring my kids or book family haircuts together?

+

Absolutely! Jennifer regularly cuts hair for women, men, teens, and children. You can request back-to-back family appointments, and waiting family members have a comfortable, quiet seating area to relax or browse with high-speed Wi-Fi.

What payment methods are accepted?

+

For your convenience, cash, Venmo, credit/debit cards, and contactless mobile pay (Apple Pay) are all accepted upon completion of your visit.

Are consultations required prior to dimensional color?

+

Every color appointment begins with an in-depth consultation right in the chair. If you are planning a major color transformation or corrective color, you can also request a quick complimentary text or in-person consultation first to review your hair history and set expectations.
